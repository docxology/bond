For the flagship recovery profile the model computes a no-decompression limit of 163.18 minutes at the working depth, an intact escort screen rated ok, and an optimal submersible cruise range of 407.61 nautical miles. Every value is derived from deterministic math over explicit parameters (dive planning, Bühlmann decompression, air consumption, convoy integrity, escort coverage, submersible logistics, range and endurance, deterministic mission software) — no random draws, no wall-clock dependence, and a fully provenanced result.
