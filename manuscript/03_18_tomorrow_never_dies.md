## Abstract (imported from the package manuscript)

**Tomorrow Never Dies: CARVER — Special-Agent Mission Software: CARVER — Special-Agent Mission Software** (*Disinformation Propagation, Stealth-Vessel Detection, and GPS-Denial Impact Analysis*). CARVER is a deterministic mission-software package that turns the media-warfare playbook of Elliot Carver into three quantitative, reproducible analyses. A disinformation model simulates how a global broadcast campaign converts a social network to a manufactured belief: under the canonical scenario, 61.7% of agents believe the message by the end of the campaign. A radar model quantifies how a low-RCS stealth hull evades detection, shrinking the detection range against a 20.0 dB RCS reduction to 57.5 km. And a GPS-denial model shows how a single jammer degrades navigation — position error grows from 6.2 m to 17.9 m — and cascades through a maritime dependency network, failing 0.429 of the fleet's systems. Keywords: disinformation propagation, media manipulation, radar cross-section, stealth detection, GPS denial, navigation failure cascade, deterministic simulation, reproducible research.
