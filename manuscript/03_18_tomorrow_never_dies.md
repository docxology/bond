## Abstract (imported from the package manuscript)

**Tomorrow Never Dies: CARVER — Special-Agent Mission Software: CARVER — Special-Agent Mission Software** (*Disinformation Propagation, Stealth-Vessel Detection, and GPS-Denial Impact Analysis*). CARVER is a deterministic mission-software package that turns the media-warfare playbook of Elliot Carver into six quantitative, reproducible analyses. A disinformation model simulates how a broadcast campaign converts a social network to a manufactured belief — 61.7% by campaign end — and a bounded-confidence narrative model shows how a *selective* broadcast pulls the audience mean 54.2% toward the manufactured line while fragmenting it into echo chambers (bimodal split 0.402; the tipping point is a reach of just 17.9%). A radar model finds that a 20.0 dB low-RCS reduction drops a stealth hull's detection range to 40.7 km, but pulse compression (27.0 dB, 15.0 m resolution, -13.5 dB range sidelobes) restores it to 192.3 km. A GPS-denial model degrades navigation error from 6.2 m to 17.9 m and fails 0.429 of the maritime dependency network; its RAIM guardian bounds the damage (HPL 15.4 m) and detects a 20 m corrupted navigation measurement with 65.9% probability. Keywords: disinformation propagation, media manipulation, radar cross-section, stealth detection, GPS denial, navigation failure cascade, deterministic simulation, reproducible research.
